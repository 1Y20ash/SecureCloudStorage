How SecureVault Works

Four connected layers for securely managing legal and investigation documents from intake to retrieval.

01

Organize by case

Create a case and classify documents by investigation, legal, forensic, evidence, or other approved categories.

02

Protect

AES-256-GCM protects stored documents while SHA-256 supports document integrity verification.

03

Control access

Role- and case-aware authorization limits sensitive operations to users who are permitted to perform them.

04

Track & retrieve

Audit activity, evidence history, versions, signatures, and document search provide traceable retrieval workflows.

Security Principles

Built For Sensitive Documents

Security remains the foundation of the DMS while case management, evidence tracking, and retrieval build on top of it.

✓

AES-256-GCM Authenticated Encryption

Stored document content is protected with authenticated encryption to provide confidentiality and tamper detection.

✓

Case-Aware Access Control

Deny-by-default authorization scopes sensitive case and document operations to permitted users and roles.

✓

Integrity & Auditability

SHA-256 integrity records and audit events help make document activity traceable and tamper-evident.

✓

Evidence & Lifecycle Controls

Versioning, evidence custody, signatures, OCR/search, and security monitoring support controlled document workflows.
